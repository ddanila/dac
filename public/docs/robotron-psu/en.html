Which power supply does this cover?

The electrical reference is drawing 56-330-2770-5 for the PC 1715. The museum machine is a W/M specimen; its enclosed PSU electronics and exact revision have not been matched to this drawing. Compare board numbers, component population and wiring before applying a reference designator. A similar enclosure does not establish circuit identity.

PSU002 photographs

Owner photographs show the separate PSU002 assembly. The owner reports that it is otherwise identical to PSU001 and that one BU126 transistor is missing. The missing part has not yet been assigned a reference designator; this does not change the transistor list in the historical drawing.

The connector board is visibly marked 105-330-2757-7, with 5N, 12N, 12P, 5P and 0V labels. These views support comparison of component placement and visible copper with the drawings. They do not yet establish circuit identity with the installed museum supply.

Rails and connector names

Keep the net labels unchanged in every language. “P” denotes a positive rail, “N” a negative rail. 12PM is the monitor supply; 12P is its separately switched output. The primary return is not the secondary 0 V.

Net Reference function Connector / value
5P Logic supply XNT 5, 6 · +5 V
12PM Monitor supply before V18 X MONU 2 / X6 3 · +12 V
12P Switched drive supply after V18 and F3 XNT 3, 4 · +12 V
5N Negative supply XNT 1 · −5 V
12N Negative supply XNT 2 · −12 V
0 V Secondary return XNT 7, 8, 9; X MONU 1 / X6 2

These are schematic pin numbers, not a mating-face drawing. Confirm connector orientation by tracing a disconnected assembly. The monitor connector’s third contact is shown as protective earth/shield; do not treat it as an interchangeable load return. Connector names can recur elsewhere in the computer.

How to read the circuit

Read the power paths and the feedback paths separately. V5 and V15 are primary switching transistors; V18 is a secondary PNP series switch. A modern transistor suitable for V18 therefore says nothing about suitability at V5. The 5P section is a forward converter; the 12PM section is a flyback converter. Negative outputs depend on loading of 5P. The 12P delay belongs to the real supply, not the emulator’s power animation.

Reference values and interpretation

The following are reference checks, not an instruction to adjust an unidentified or faulty unit. The original procedure supplies the complete loading conditions and order. Mains-side measurements and repairs require appropriate isolation, probes and trained handling; C3 can retain hazardous voltage after unplugging. An earthed oscilloscope ground must not be attached to the primary return.

Mains input 220 V; 47–63 Hz; 187–242 V §1.9.3 · 93 / PDF 95

A reading of about 12 V on DISPLAY does not prove that 12P is present. Conversely, missing 12P does not by itself prove V18 failed: F3, the drive stage, undervoltage detection and delay circuitry are distinct possibilities. Inspect them as separate hypotheses. Do not bridge F3 or V18 to make the symptom disappear. A no-load reading on a negative rail cannot establish a regulator fault.

Soviet and modern replacement candidates

No substitution below has been tested in this museum PSU. “Candidate” means worth evaluating in the identified circuit, not a verified repair or a pin-compatible equivalent. The most defensible starting point is V18, because the reference itself already specifies the Soviet КТ818А.

Position / candidate Evidence Decision and remaining checks
V18 · КТ818Б (KT818B) Same Soviet type family. INTEGRAL specifies 10 A, 60 W at case ≤25 °C, and h21э ≥20 for Б versus ≥15 for А at its stated test point. [kt818] A strong family candidate, subject to the exact maker/package datasheet, authenticity and condition. Verify saturation at the available base drive, mounting and switch-off timing. The Cyrillic Б is transliterated B; Cyrillic В is V. Do not confuse the grades.
V18 · ST TIP36C PNP; |VCEO| 100 V, |IC| 25 A, 125 W at case 25 °C; TO-247. ST lists the part active. [tip36] [tip36-status] Modern candidate requiring a mounting/wiring review. INTEGRAL КТ818 uses 1=E, 2=C, 3=B; TIP36C uses 1=B, 2=C, 3=E. Do not fit it by matching pin positions. Absolute ratings do not prove adequate gain, safe operating area or low voltage drop at 3–5 A in this driver.
V5 / V15 · onsemi FJL6920 NPN; VCEO 800 V, IC 20 A, 200 W at case 25 °C; TO-264. Gain at 11 A is only 5.5–8.5; saturation is specified with 2.75 A base drive. [fjl] Comparison example only; NOT approved as an SU169 replacement. Its larger headline ratings do not establish compatibility with Tr101/Tr102. Storage time, reverse base drive, safe operating area and mounting need circuit-specific validation. No Soviet or modern replacement is qualified here for these primary switches.

For SF358, SD335B, SD336B, SC236D and SF127C, this edition deliberately leaves the replacement open until original manufacturer data and the actual circuit stresses are compared. A cross-reference search result is not enough. Retain gain-group suffixes. A MOSFET or Darlington is not a direct substitute for an ordinary bipolar transistor in this circuit.

What would make a substitution verified?

Record the actual PSU assembly/revision, removed part marking, replacement manufacturer and lot, lead-to-net mapping and insulation. Verify turn-on and turn-off sequencing, saturation drop, dissipation and temperature over the specified loads and mains range. Check startup, transient load and protection behaviour with suitable test equipment. For primary switches, include collector and base waveforms and safe-operating-area margins. Attach measured results before changing a candidate to “bench-qualified”. No such measurements were made for this publication.

Verification and unresolved points

The schematic, both placement drawings and the service check pages were inspected as images; OCR was used only as a navigation aid. Numerical tables are shared across the three editions. The guide preserves the distinction between printed and PDF page numbers. The service text at §1.9.8.1 refers to V12 as a power switch, whereas the circuit identifies V15 and places V12 among the rectifier diodes: use the drawing to resolve that discrepancy. Translation and source review were performed in one editorial pass; there has been no independent human technical review or hardware validation.
